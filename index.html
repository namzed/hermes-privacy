Hermes — Privacy Statement

Last updated 9 November 2026

Hermes is a private, single-user automation assistant. It is operated by Derek Nguyen for his own personal use — email, calendar, and files. It is not a product, has no other users, and is not distributed or offered to the public.

What it accesses

Only the Google account its owner authorises, and only the scopes he approves: Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive. Access is used solely to read and organise the owner's own mail, appointments and files on his behalf.

Where data goes

Nowhere. Data is processed on a private server controlled by the owner. Nothing is sold, shared, published or transferred to any third party. No analytics, no advertising, no profiling.

Storage

Credentials are stored on the owner's own private server with restricted file permissions. Email content and files are not copied to any external service.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the assistant's access to the account.
